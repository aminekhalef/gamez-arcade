Play Tunisian Chkobba online

Chkobba is Tunisia's classic fishing card game, played here with the traditional 40-card deck. Choose solo practice against the café rival or create a synchronized private room for two players.

How to play Tunisian Chkobba

  1. Play one card each turn and capture an equal-ranked table card when one is available.
  2. When there is no equal-ranked card, capture a valid combination whose values add up to the played card.
  3. Clearing the table during normal play scores a Chkobba sweep.
  4. At the end of a round, points include the most cards, most diamonds, Bermila, the seven of diamonds and earned Chkobba sweeps.
  5. The match ends when a player reaches 11 points with the required winning margin.

Controls

  • Choose a card from your hand. If a capture exists, select the matching table card or a combination that totals its value, then confirm the play.
  • If no capture is available, play the selected card onto the table. In an online room, only the active player can move.

Scoring and game format

Round points include most cards, most diamonds (Dinari), Bermila, the seven of diamonds (7aya), and one point per Chkobba sweep. A match is won at 11 or more points with a two-point lead.

Play solo against the café rival or open a synchronized private room for exactly two players.

Tunisian Chkobba FAQ

Which cards are in the Chkobba deck?
The game uses a 40-card Tunisian deck: Ace through 7 plus Jack, Queen and King in each suit.
When does clearing the table count as Chkobba?
A capture that clears the table scores a sweep during normal play, but the final play of the deal does not.
