Play Tunisian Chkobba online

Français

Chkobba is Tunisia's classic fishing card game, played here with the traditional 40-card deck. Play Chkobba Café against the computer or host a private online table with friends and bots.

How to play Tunisian Chkobba

  1. Play one card each turn. In a private room, take all cards of the same value if there are any. Otherwise, take a combination that adds up to the value of your card if one is available.
  2. In solo mode, choose any available capture that adds up to your card. If there is no valid capture, leave your card on the table.
  3. Clearing the table during normal play scores a Chkobba sweep.
  4. At the end of a round, points include the most cards, most diamonds, Bermila, the seven of diamonds and earned Chkobba sweeps.
  5. In solo mode, the match ends when a player reaches the chosen target and leads at the end of a round. In private rooms, a player must reach at least 11 points and lead by two.

Controls

  • Choose a card from your hand. If a capture exists, select the matching table card or a combination that totals its value, then confirm the play.
  • If no capture is available, play the selected card onto the table.

Scoring and game format

Round points include most cards, most diamonds (Dinari), Bermila, the seven of diamonds (7aya), and one point per Chkobba sweep. Bermila goes to whoever captures the most sevens, with sixes breaking the tie.

Choose Play online, share your six-character room code, and select two, three or four seats. Bots fill empty seats when the host starts. Every seat scores individually.

Tunisian Chkobba FAQ

Which cards are in the Chkobba deck?
The game uses a 40-card Tunisian deck: Ace through 7 plus Jack, Queen and King in each suit.
When does clearing the table count as Chkobba?
A capture that clears the table scores a sweep during normal play, but the final play of the round does not.
Can I start an online Chkobba match with fewer than four people?
Yes. Set the table to two, three or four seats. Any open seats are filled by bots at the start of the match.
