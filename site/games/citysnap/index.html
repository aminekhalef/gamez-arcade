GAME GUIDE

Play citySnap online

citySnap is a visual city quiz built around recognizable landmarks, streets and landscapes. Study each image, search the city list and submit the destination you think it shows.

How to play citySnap

  1. Inspect the photograph for landmarks, architecture, terrain and other geographic clues.
  2. Type a city name and choose the intended result from the suggestions.
  3. Submit the answer to reveal whether the city is correct.
  4. Work through the image set and build the strongest score you can.

Controls

  • Study the photo, type a city or country, and use the autocomplete list if you want to choose a precise city result.
  • Submit the answer, review the reveal, and continue through all five photos. The optional hint can be opened before guessing.

Scoring and game format

An exact city earns 5,000 points, the correct country earns 2,500, and a wrong answer earns zero. Five rounds make a maximum score of 25,000.

citySnap is a single-player photo quiz and does not use online rooms.

citySnap FAQ

Can I answer with only the country?
Yes. A correct country earns partial credit even when the city is not identified.
How many photos are in one run?
Each run randomly selects five locations from the game's photo collection.
