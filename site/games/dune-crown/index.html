GAME GUIDE

Play Dune Crown online

Dune Crown is a compact turn-based desert strategy game. Build an army, manage water income, claim oases and destroy every rival citadel on a 13-by-13 tactical map.

How to play Dune Crown

  1. Recruit scouts, guards, archers, knights and catapults by spending water.
  2. Move and attack once per unit each turn; dunes cost extra movement and rocks block the path.
  3. Claim oases for additional income and healing, then reduce rival citadels from 12 health to zero.
  4. Online opponents each face the same two-player AI setup; the first to deal all 12 citadel damage wins.

Controls

  • Select a unit, then choose a highlighted movement or attack tile. Recruit buttons show valid placement cells near your citadel.
  • Use End Turn after finishing your actions. The solo setup supports two to four local human or AI players.

Scoring and game format

Solo victory goes to the last surviving citadel. Online progress measures damage dealt to the mirrored AI citadel, with 12 damage required to win.

Dune Crown supports pass-and-play solo setup options and private two-player online mirrored siege challenges.

Dune Crown FAQ

How do oases work?
A unit ending its turn on an oasis claims it. Owned oases add water income and heal friendly units standing there.
Why is online Dune Crown a mirrored challenge?
Both players receive the same objective independently, which keeps turns responsive while still synchronizing progress and the winner through the room server.
