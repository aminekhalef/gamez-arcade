GAME GUIDE

Play Harba! online

Harba is an automatic runner inspired by Tunisia. Cross five increasingly difficult districts, collect dinars and a unique Tunisian booster in every level, avoid street hazards and keep enough distance from the pursuing patrol to complete the one-kilometre escape.

How to play Harba!

  1. The character runs automatically while the street and hazards become faster in each district.
  2. Jump or double-jump over crates, baskets, scooters and carts, and slide under tall barriers.
  3. Collect dinars, Brik shields and temporary Harissa turbo boosts along the route.
  4. A collision reduces the lead over the patrol; the run ends if the officer catches up.
  5. In an online duel, both players race live and the first to reach one kilometre wins.

Controls

  • Press Space, W or Up to jump, then press again in the air for a double jump.
  • Hold S or Down to slide. On touchscreens, use the separate jump and slide buttons.

Scoring and game format

Distance is the main score and dinars are collected during the run. Online rooms synchronize both runners toward the one-kilometre finish.

Harba supports solo play and private two-player online distance races using a six-character room code.

Harba! FAQ

What do the Harba power-ups do?
The Brik shield absorbs one collision, while Harissa turbo temporarily increases running speed and protects the player from a crash.
Is online Harba real-time?
Yes. Both players run on their own device while the room scoreboard synchronizes distance and declares the first to one kilometre the winner.
