GAME GUIDE

Play Harba! online

Harba is an automatic runner inspired by Tunisia. Escape through Sidi Bou Said, The Souk, Tunis by Night, La Marsa Corniche, and Bab Bhar. Collect dinars, buy boosts at stalls, and tip carts to slow the pursuing patrol.

How to play Harba!

  1. The character runs automatically while the street and hazards become faster in each district.
  2. Jump over crates, benches, and bollards, climb walls, and slide under hanging laundry.
  3. Collect dinars and boosts along the route. When the boulis spot you, tip a cart into their path to break the sprint.
  4. A collision reduces the lead over the patrol; the run ends if the officer catches up.
  5. In an online duel, the first player to finish all five stages wins. If both are caught, the farther runner wins.

Controls

  • Press Space, W or Up to jump, then press again in the air for a double jump.
  • Hold S or Down to slide, hold Up at a wall to climb, and press E to tip carts or buy at stalls. On touchscreens, tap low on the game to slide and use the Jump and Act buttons.

Scoring and game format

Collect dinars as you run. In an online duel, completing all five stages earns 1,000 race points.

Harba supports solo play and private two-player online distance races using a six-character room code.

Harba! FAQ

What do the Harba power-ups do?
The Brik shield absorbs one collision, while Harissa turbo temporarily increases running speed and protects the player from a crash.
Is online Harba real-time?
Yes. Both players run on their own device and the room scoreboard updates their progress as they play.
