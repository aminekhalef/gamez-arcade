GAME GUIDE

Play HowMuch? online

HowMuch? tests your sense of value across a varied collection of products and objects. Enter an estimate, reveal the real price and see how close your instincts were.

How to play HowMuch?

  1. Review the item and its supporting details before making an estimate.
  2. Enter your price using the on-screen controls and submit the guess.
  3. Your result is based on the distance between the estimate and the listed price.
  4. Continue through the challenge set and try to improve the overall score.

Controls

  • Use the numeric keypad or price slider to enter a dollar estimate, then confirm it to reveal the listed price.
  • Continue after each reveal to complete all ten items and review the round-by-round results.

Scoring and game format

Each of ten rounds awards up to 5,000 points based on the ratio between your estimate and the listed price, for a maximum total of 50,000.

HowMuch? is a single-player browser game and does not use an online room.

HowMuch? FAQ

How is a HowMuch? guess scored?
Closer proportional estimates earn more points, whether the guess is above or below the listed price.
Which currency does the game use?
Prices and guesses are shown in US dollars.
