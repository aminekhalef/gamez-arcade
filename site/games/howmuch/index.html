GAME GUIDE

Play HowMuch? online

HowMuch? tests your sense of value across a varied collection of products and objects. Enter an estimate, reveal the real price and see how close your instincts were.

How to play HowMuch?

  1. Review the item and its supporting details before making an estimate.
  2. Enter your price using the on-screen controls and submit the guess.
  3. Your result is based on the distance between the estimate and the listed price.
  4. Continue through the challenge set and try to improve the overall score.
