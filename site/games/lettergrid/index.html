Lettergrid is a five-letter word puzzle. You have six guesses, and each guess reveals which letters are in the hidden word and where they fit.

How to play Lettergrid

  1. Enter a valid five-letter word for each guess.
  2. Green means the letter is in the right spot. Yellow means it belongs in a different spot. Gray means there is no matching copy left.
  3. Use the clues to find the hidden word within six guesses.

Controls

  • Type using your physical keyboard or tap the letters on the screen.
  • Use Enter to submit your guess and Backspace to remove the last letter.

Scoring and game format

Solve the puzzle in as few guesses as you can. Open Stats to see your daily results and streak.

Play the daily puzzle or choose Unlimited for more solo rounds.
