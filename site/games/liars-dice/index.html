Play Liar's Dice online

Dudo Table is a four-seat version of Liar's Dice. Play solo against three computer opponents, or share a room code with friends and let the computer fill the open seats.

How to play Liar's Dice

  1. Each player starts a round with concealed dice that only they can see.
  2. On your turn, raise the current bid by increasing its quantity or face value.
  3. Challenge when you believe the previous bid is higher than the dice on the table can support.
  4. Bid on faces 2 through 6. Paco jokers count toward any bid in normal rounds. In a Palifico round, only dice showing the bid face count.
  5. The last player with dice remaining wins the match.

Controls

  • Play solo as soon as you open the table, or select Play online to create or join a private room.
  • Use the quantity and face controls to make a higher bid, or choose Dudo to challenge the current bid.

Scoring and game format

There is no separate points total. A failed bid or incorrect challenge costs one die, and the final player with at least one die wins.

Select Play online, create a room and share its six-character code. Friends join with that code. When the host starts, computer opponents fill any open seats at the four-player table.

Liar's Dice FAQ

Can I play Liar's Dice online with just one friend?
Yes. Share your private room code with your friend and start once they join. Two computer opponents fill the remaining seats.
When are the dice revealed?
The table reveals its dice when a player calls Dudo to challenge the current bid.
