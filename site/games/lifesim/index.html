GAME GUIDE

Play LifeSim online

LifeSim is a choice-driven life simulation. Age one year at a time, respond to events and balance health, happiness, intelligence, money and karma across a complete life story.

How to play LifeSim

  1. Name your character and begin at birth with a unique starting set of attributes.
  2. Advance one year at a time and choose how to respond when a life event appears.
  3. Meet career requirements to take a job and earn its annual salary.
  4. Balance your attributes while collecting achievements and building a personal timeline.

Controls

  • Use the on-screen buttons to age up, choose event responses, select a career and review your timeline.
  • Progress is saved in this browser so an unfinished life can be continued after a reload.

Scoring and game format

LifeSim has no single winning score. Progress is measured through attributes, wealth, career growth, experience levels and unlocked achievements.

LifeSim is a single-player simulation and does not use online rooms.

LifeSim FAQ

Does LifeSim save my progress?
Yes. The current life is stored locally in your browser and can be continued from the start screen.
Are all event outcomes guaranteed?
No. Some events and outcomes are random, while your choices directly change the listed attributes.
