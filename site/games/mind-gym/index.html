GAME GUIDE

Play Mind Gym online

Mind Gym is a compact set of solo brain-training exercises built for quick repeatable sessions. Choose a discipline, react accurately and try to beat the best score saved in your browser.

How to play Mind Gym

  1. Choose one of the available mental-training exercises.
  2. Follow the prompt and answer as accurately and quickly as possible.
  3. Finish the session to record a score for that exercise.
  4. Replay an exercise to improve the locally saved best score.

Controls

  • Use the on-screen buttons or keyboard prompts shown by each exercise.
  • Choose another exercise from the results screen or replay the current challenge to improve your best score.

Scoring and game format

Each exercise calculates its own performance score and stores the best result locally in your browser.

Mind Gym is a single-player browser game.

Mind Gym FAQ

Does Mind Gym save my high scores?
Yes. Best scores are saved locally in the browser on this device.
Do I need to install anything?
No. Mind Gym runs directly in the browser.
