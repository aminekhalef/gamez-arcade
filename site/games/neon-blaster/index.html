GAME GUIDE

Play Neon Blaster online

Neon Blaster is a fast Bomberman-style action game rendered on canvas. Navigate breakable mazes, time bomb blasts, defeat enemies and improve your abilities with dropped power-ups.

How to play Neon Blaster

  1. Move through the maze and place bombs in open grid cells.
  2. Stay outside each bomb's horizontal and vertical blast range when its timer expires.
  3. Destroy breakable walls to open routes and reveal power-ups.
  4. Defeat every enemy to clear the level; losing all lives ends the run.

Controls

  • Use WASD or the arrow keys to move and Space or Enter to place a bomb.
  • Press P or Escape to pause. Phones and tablets can use the directional pad and bomb button below the canvas.

Scoring and game format

Defeating enemies and chaining explosions adds points and combo bonuses. Clearing a level advances to a harder maze while keeping earned upgrades.

Neon Blaster is currently a single-player browser game.

Neon Blaster FAQ

What do Neon Blaster power-ups do?
Power-ups can increase bomb capacity, blast range or movement speed, and can grant temporary shield or ghost effects.
Can Neon Blaster be paused?
Yes. Press P or Escape, or use the pause control in the game HUD.
