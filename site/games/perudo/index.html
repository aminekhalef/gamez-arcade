GAME GUIDE

Play Liar's Dice online

Liar's Dice is a live multiplayer bluffing game for two to four players. Create a private room, share its six-character code and try to read the table while every player's dice remain hidden.

How to play Liar's Dice

  1. Each player starts a round with concealed dice that only they can see.
  2. On your turn, raise the current bid by increasing its quantity or face value.
  3. Challenge when you believe the previous bid is higher than the dice on the table can support.
  4. After a challenge, ones count as wild unless the bid itself is for ones; the losing player gives up a die.
  5. The last player with dice remaining wins the match.

Controls

  • Create or join a private room, then wait for the host to start once at least two players are seated.
  • Use the quantity and face controls to make a higher bid, or choose Dudo to challenge the current bid.

Scoring and game format

There is no separate points total. A failed bid or incorrect challenge costs one die, and the final player with at least one die wins.

Online multiplayer supports two to four players in a private six-character room. There is no solo mode.

Liar's Dice FAQ

How many people can play Liar's Dice?
Private rooms support two, three or four players.
Can other players see my dice?
No. Your roll remains private until a challenge makes the server reveal and count the table.
