GAME GUIDE

Play Stadt, Land, Fluss online

Stadt, Land, Fluss is a five-round category word game. A letter is chosen for each round and every answer must begin with it. Play locally in German, English or Arabic, or enter a private online score duel.

How to play Stadt, Land, Fluss

  1. Select at least three categories, then begin a five-round game.
  2. Enter one valid answer per category before the 90-second timer ends.
  3. Every answer must begin with the displayed letter.
  4. Review the round and reject any answer that should not count.
  5. In an online duel, both players receive the same five-letter sequence and the higher final score wins.

Controls

  • Type into each category field, press Enter to move forward, and use Stop when the round is complete.
  • Review each submitted answer after the timer and reject entries that do not fit the letter or category before continuing.

Scoring and game format

Valid answers earn 10 points each, plus a time bonus for completing every selected category early. Online duels compare the final score after five rounds.

Play solo in German, English or Arabic, or create a private synchronized two-player room with a six-character code.

Stadt, Land, Fluss FAQ

Can I play Stadt, Land, Fluss alone?
Yes. Solo mode contains the complete five-round game and saves your best score locally.
What is Bent w Wled?
It is the Tunisian category variant with girl, boy, animal, plant, object and country.
