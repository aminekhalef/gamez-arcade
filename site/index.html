Gamez Hub: 13 free online arcade games

Play 13 free online games at Gamez Hub. Enjoy arcade action, Tunisian Chkobba, multiplayer tables, word games and more, directly in your browser. Nothing needs to be installed: choose a solo challenge or open a private multiplayer room and start playing.

  • Liar's Dice

    Liar's Dice is a live multiplayer bluffing game for two to four players. Create a private room, share its six-character code and try to read the table while every player's dice remain hidden.

  • Vache Taureau

    Vache Taureau is a head-to-head number deduction game. Both players lock a secret four-digit code, then exchange guesses and use exact and misplaced matches to solve the rival code first.

  • Tunisian Chkobba

    Chkobba is Tunisia's classic fishing card game, played here with the traditional 40-card deck. Choose solo practice against the café rival or create a synchronized private room for two players.

  • HowMuch?

    HowMuch? tests your sense of value across a varied collection of products and objects. Enter an estimate, reveal the real price and see how close your instincts were.

  • MapTap

    MapTap turns geography clues into globe-based challenges. Interpret the prompt, rotate the map and place your answer close to the target to earn more points.

  • citySnap

    citySnap is a visual city quiz built around recognizable landmarks, streets and landscapes. Study each image, search the city list and submit the destination you think it shows.

  • RiddleGame

    RiddleGame is a single-player puzzle challenge with selectable difficulty levels. Solve each prompt, manage limited lives and build a scoring streak across a complete run.

  • LifeSim

    LifeSim is a choice-driven life simulation. Age one year at a time, respond to events and balance health, happiness, intelligence, money and karma across a complete life story.

  • Neon Blaster

    Neon Blaster is a fast Bomberman-style action game rendered on canvas. Navigate breakable mazes, time bomb blasts, defeat enemies and improve your abilities with dropped power-ups.

  • Harba!

    Harba is an automatic runner inspired by Tunisia. Escape through Sidi Bou Said, The Souk, Tunis by Night, La Marsa Corniche, and Bab Bhar. Collect dinars, buy boosts at stalls, and tip carts to slow the pursuing patrol.

  • Dune Crown

    Dune Crown is a compact turn-based desert strategy game. Build an army, manage water income, claim oases and destroy every rival citadel on a 13-by-13 tactical map.

  • Mind Gym

    Mind Gym is a compact set of solo brain-training exercises built for quick repeatable sessions. Choose a discipline, react accurately and try to beat the best score saved in your browser.

  • Stadt, Land, Fluss

    Stadt, Land, Fluss is a five-round category word game. A letter is chosen for each round and every answer must begin with it. Play locally in German, English or Arabic, or enter a private online score duel.

Find a game to play

Try citySnap if you like recognizing landmarks, or play a quick round of Mind Gym to test your memory and reflexes. For an arcade challenge, dodge obstacles in Harba! or clear a maze in Neon Blaster.

Play with friends in a private room

Open an online game such as Tunisian Chkobba or Liar’s Dice, enter your name and create a room. Share the six-character code with your friends. They can open the same game, enter the code and choose Join room.
