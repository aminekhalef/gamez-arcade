PLAY IN YOUR BROWSER

Eleven free online arcade games

Play eleven free browser games in Gamez Room Arcade, including multiplayer tables, runners, strategy, geography, trivia and riddles. Nothing needs to be installed: choose a solo challenge or open a private multiplayer room and start playing.

  • Liar's DiceLiar's Dice is a live multiplayer bluffing game for two to four players. Create a private room, share its six-character code and try to read the table while every player's dice remain hidden.
  • Vache TaureauVache Taureau is a head-to-head number deduction game. Both players lock a secret four-digit code, then exchange guesses and use exact and misplaced matches to solve the rival code first.
  • Tunisian ChkobbaChkobba is Tunisia's classic fishing card game, played here with the traditional 40-card deck. Choose solo practice against the café rival or create a synchronized private room for two players.
  • HowMuch?HowMuch? tests your sense of value across a varied collection of products and objects. Enter an estimate, reveal the real price and see how close your instincts were.
  • MapTapMapTap turns geography clues into globe-based challenges. Interpret the prompt, rotate the map and place your answer close to the target to earn more points.
  • citySnapcitySnap is a visual city quiz built around recognizable landmarks, streets and landscapes. Study each image, search the city list and submit the destination you think it shows.
  • RiddleGameRiddleGame is a single-player puzzle challenge with selectable difficulty levels. Solve each prompt, manage limited lives and build a scoring streak across a complete run.
  • LifeSimLifeSim is a choice-driven life simulation. Age one year at a time, respond to events and balance health, happiness, intelligence, money and karma across a complete life story.
  • Neon BlasterNeon Blaster is a fast Bomberman-style action game rendered on canvas. Navigate breakable mazes, time bomb blasts, defeat enemies and improve your abilities with dropped power-ups.
  • Harba!Harba is an automatic runner inspired by Tunisia. Cross five increasingly difficult districts, collect dinars and a unique Tunisian booster in every level, avoid street hazards and keep enough distance from the pursuing patrol to complete the one-kilometre escape.
  • Dune CrownDune Crown is a compact turn-based desert strategy game. Build an army, manage water income, claim oases and destroy every rival citadel on a 13-by-13 tactical map.
