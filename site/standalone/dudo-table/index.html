    How to play

    Be the last player with dice. Each round, everyone rolls in secret. A bid like four 5s claims there are at least four dice matching 5 across all cups.

    Raise the bid

    Bid on a face from 2 to 6 in every round. Increase the quantity and choose any number, or keep the quantity and choose a higher number. During a normal round, the Paco joker counts toward the bid.

    Call Dudo

    Challenge the last bid. If enough dice match, the challenger loses one die. Otherwise, the bidder loses one. The player who loses a die opens the next round, or the next surviving seat opens if they’re eliminated.

    Play a Palifico round

    The first time a player drops to one die, they open a Palifico round. Only dice showing the bid number count. The face is fixed, except a player who completed an earlier Palifico can raise to a different face. Palifico is disabled heads-up.

    Start a new game?

    This will end your current match and give everyone five dice.
