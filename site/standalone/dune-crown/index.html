🏜️ Dune Crown

Seize the oases, raise an army, topple the rival citadels.

How to play

Goal: destroy every rival citadel 🏰 (12 HP each). The last player standing wins.

On your turn

  • Recruit: tap a unit button, then a dashed yellow tile within 2 tiles of your citadel. New units can’t act this turn.
  • Move: tap one of your units. Green tiles show where it can go.
  • Attack: red tiles are targets in range (enemy units or citadels). A unit can move, then attack, once per turn.
  • End turn when you’re done. Turns pass clockwise: Blue, Red, Green, Gold.

Map and economy

  • 🌴 Oasis: end your turn standing on one to claim it. You earn 2 💧 per turn, plus 2 💧 for each oasis you hold. Others can steal it the same way.
  • ≈ Dune: costs 2 moves to enter. A unit standing on one takes 1 less damage (minimum 1).
  • 🪨 Rock: blocks movement. Units can’t pass through each other.
  • You start with 5 💧. The map is 13×13 with four corner citadels and a central oasis.
  • 🩹 Units standing on an oasis you hold heal 1 HP at the start of your turn.
  • 🏰 Your citadel shoots 1 damage at enemies within 2 tiles at the start of your turn.
  • ⭐ A unit that makes a kill gains +1 attack and heals 2 HP (first two kills).
  • 🌫️ Fog of war (optional): you only see tiles within 3 of your units, 4 of your citadel and 1 of your oases. With several humans, a “pass the device” screen hides the board between turns. The AI ignores fog.
  • 🤖 AI level: Easy sometimes misses attacks and builds fewer units. Hard gets +2 💧 per turn and recruits stronger units.
  • 🔊 The speaker button toggles sound.

2 to 4 players

  • Pick 2, 3 or 4 players in the setup. Each slot can be Human or AI. Humans share one device and pass it on.
  • There are no alliances: everyone can attack everyone.
  • When a citadel falls, that player is out, their units vanish and their oases become free.

Tips

  • Grab nearby oases early. Archers behind Guards are strong. In 3 or 4 player games, let rivals weaken each other first.
