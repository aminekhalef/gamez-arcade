    How to play

    Jump over obstacles and press again in the air for a double jump. Hold Up at a wall to climb. Hold Down to slide under laundry. Press E near a cart or stall to act.

    On touchscreens, use the three buttons. You can also tap the upper part of the game to jump, or hold the bottom to slide. Solo runs pause when you leave the window. Press P to pause or resume.
